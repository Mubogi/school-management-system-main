## 2. Problem & Target Market
- **Problem:** African schools need school management that works without internet and can't be pirated.
- **Target users:** Primary/secondary schools in Uganda and Africa.
- **Market context:** Offline-first with hardware-bound (HWID) licensing to prevent unauthorized copying.

## 3. Value Proposition & Features
- Students & teachers management
- Fees tracking
- Marks/grades & reports
- Parent communications
- Single-device HWID licensing
- Offline-first operation

## 4. Business / Monetization Model
- **Pricing:** license per school
- **Revenue streams:** Per-school licenses (HWID-bound)
- **Payment methods:** Direct arrangement
